# Product: Copy-fox

## Problem
A fox-lover wants to play with fox models and copy monochrome text foxes into Telegram.

## Success metric
Select a fox, compose a view, and copy readable art in less than one minute during a browser smoke check.

## Announcement
Copy-fox turns foxes into text. Rotate a fox, zoom in, and choose ASCII, blocks, or Braille. Pick a terminal palette and a text size. Animated foxes can move until you pause or copy a frame. Paste the result into Telegram and apply monospace formatting.

## Screens
One working screen with an interactive text preview and settings. Desktop uses two columns. Phones use a stacked layout.
